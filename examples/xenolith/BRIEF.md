# Xenolith / Basilisk production brief

## Xenolith — primary exhibit

Subject: fictional non-human cognition. Money shot: a monumental alien containment
engine unfolds its inner rotors around a suspended geometric signal, while fine
streams and optical fields move through detailed metallic architecture.

Palette/materials: dark blue absorptive mass, cold metallic edges, ice emission,
copper structural accents, warm signal paths, translucent spectral surfaces.
Shape vocabulary: articulated radial shrouds, layered housings, counter-rotating
interior optics, filament buses, peripheral sensor assemblies. Dense small parts
are instanced or merged; the whole scene occupies the viewport.

Performance: acquire → reconstruct → converge → ignition → transmit → recover
over 24 seconds. GPU fields, traces, peripheral instruments, and slow camera motion
continue throughout. Opening enters the developed sequence at nine seconds.

## Basilisk — finance transfer study

Subject: simulated market microstructure, with no autonomous agents. Money shot:
two massive order-book strata part around a luminous price river; cross-section
planes scan through the volume while trade particles flow along the spread.

This deliberately changes the dominant geometry from radial machinery to an
oblique landscape. It reuses optics, clock, and instrument shell to demonstrate
transfer between subjects. It is not a second independent agent evaluation.

## Delivery contract

1440×900 desktop, 390×860 portrait; deterministic `?t=` frames at 0, 8, 12, 17,
22 seconds. `?world=market` selects Basilisk; `?hud=0` isolates the physical shot.
All values are simulated. Controls: scenario, selection, pause, climax trigger,
freeze URL. Reduced motion holds a developed frame. WebGL failure shows a static
composition and explicit fallback status.

The reference deliberately remains procedural and self-contained, with no paid
models, API keys, remote fonts, Blender runtime, or server-side rendering service.
GLB assets and a film-export pipeline remain optional upgrades in the skill.
