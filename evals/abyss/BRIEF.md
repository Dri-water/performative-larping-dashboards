# ABYSS / liquidity fault

Fictional trading becomes a monumental fault cut through hundreds of metallic order-book strata. A molten price ribbon winds through the spread; the opposing walls shear apart to expose a lattice of dormant bids.

Money shot: an oblique aerial view falls between jagged copper and ice terraces while an ivory torrent ruptures the canyon and the walls unfold like geological instruments.

Vocabulary: black titanium platelets, warm copper sell edges, glacial blue bid seams, flowing ivory filaments; shearing, scanning, rippling and release. One 20-second score drives architecture, trade torrents, peripheral traces, and telemetry.

Capture: 1440x900 desktop; 430x900 portrait, composed down the canyon. Opening 0, build 6, rupture 11, recovery 18 seconds. All data explicitly fictional. Three.js 0.186.1, Vite 8.3.2, Playwright 1.63.0, Node 24.14.1. Procedural geometry; no assets, agents or mascots.
